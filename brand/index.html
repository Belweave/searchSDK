Icons and cards

Voice

  • The name is always searchSDK.
  • Say what it does, in plain words. One idea per sentence.
  • Word caps: hero 30, home page 150, docs page 300 outside code, card headline 8.
  • Back every claim where it is made: a benchmark figure links its run, date and method.
  • Sample data carries a Sample data tag; an unverified fact carries Unverified.
  • Banned: seamless, effortless, revolutionary, powerful, unlock, supercharge, game-changing, next-level, blazing, lightning-fast, cutting-edge, world-class, best-in-class, magic, ultimate, robust.

Provider logos

  • Each provider's official files, unmodified, under its own rules, with the source recorded.
  • Never recoloured, redrawn, animated, cropped or given effects; never larger than the searchSDK mark.
  • Logos identify providers in comparisons. They never imply endorsement, partnership or affiliation.
  • Pages that name providers say the names are their owners' trademarks and that searchSDK is not affiliated with them.
