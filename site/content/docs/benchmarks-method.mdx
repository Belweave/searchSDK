---
title: Benchmarks method
description: How searchSDK benchmarks web search providers end to end, scores answer quality and ranks the leaderboard.
---

Every provider runs the same queries through searchSDK, end to end, with settings tuned to suit it. The harness is open, so anyone can rerun it with their own keys.

## The run

- About 400 queries, once a month.
- Each query goes to every provider with the same deadline and result limit.
- Every response, failure and timing is recorded.

## Quality

Two measures, combined into one score from 0 to 100:

- **Known-answer tasks.** Queries with a checked answer. A provider scores when its results contain it.
- **Judge panel.** Low-cost model judges grade relevance against a published rubric. People check a sample of their grades.

## Ranking

1. Providers that fail or return nothing too often are marked unreliable and ranked below every dependable one.
2. Quality decides the rest.
3. Providers within 6 points of each other are tied.

Latency and cost are reported beside the rank. Cost never changes rank.

## Funding

Runs are paid for by provider credits, donations and a capped monthly budget from the maintainer. Every source is disclosed with the results.
